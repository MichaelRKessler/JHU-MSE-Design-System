Section opener: condensed eyebrow, Heritage Blue title, short gold rule, optional lede + right action.

Provide `title` and optionally `eyebrow`, `lede` (intro paragraph under the short gold rule), `action` (a right-aligned ghost Button) and `onDark`. Opens every major section of a page or report.

```jsx
<SectionHeader eyebrow="Research" title="A Focus on Research" lede="From biomaterials to structural materials…" />
```
