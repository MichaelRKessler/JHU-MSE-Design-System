# Source files

The official JHU files that the design system was built from, kept here as-is so you can work in Office or send logos to a printer.

## templates/

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `jhu-deck-template.potx` | Official JHU PowerPoint template. The 38 line icons in `project/assets/Icons/` come from this file. |
| `jhu-flyer-templates.pptx` | Official JHU flier layouts in PowerPoint. |
| `jhu-zoom-backgrounds.pptx` | Official Zoom backgrounds, as slides. The same Heritage, Medium and Spirit Blue backgrounds are in `project/assets/Zoom Backgrounds/` as JPEGs. |
| `jhu-report-template.docx` | Official JHU Word report template. Follow the JHU Report Template Support Guide (`project/assets/Guides/jhu-report-template-guide.pdf`) when you fill it in, and pair it with the ReportCover template. |

## logos/

Complete logo kits for the university and the Whiting School of Engineering. Each has the same layout:

```
RGB (Digital)/SVG    Screens and the web; scales to any size
RGB (Digital)/PNG    Office documents and anywhere SVG isn't accepted
CMYK (Print)/JPG     Print, when the printer can't take EPS
CMYK (Print)/EPS     Professional print
```

- **Johns Hopkins University Logos:** horizontal and vertical lock-ups in blue, black and white (no white JPGs).
- **Whiting School of Engineering Logos:** horizontal and vertical lock-ups in blue, black and white (no white JPGs). `project/assets/Logos/` has the same WSE marks as SVG.

Use the blue lock-ups on white, the white ones on Heritage Blue or photography, and the black ones only where color printing is unavailable. Don't stretch, recolor or crowd them. There is no department lock-up: set "Department of Materials Science and Engineering" in Work Sans 600 beside or under the WSE mark.
